Beginner-friendly domain + email setup
Since you’re a first timer, here’s the simple version:

Step 1: buy the domain
Buy projectpergamon.ai through a registrar.

Best beginner pick:

Namecheap
Also good:

Porkbun
Cloudflare Registrar
Step 2: create Google Workspace
sign up for Google Workspace
choose use an existing domain
enter projectpergamon.ai
create support@projectpergamon.ai
Step 3: verify the domain
Google will give you a DNS record to paste into your domain settings.

Step 4: add MX records
These tell the internet that your email is handled by Google.

Step 5: add SPF, DKIM, and DMARC
These improve deliverability and reduce spam issues.

Step 6: connect the form
Your builder connects the site form so submissions:

email support@projectpergamon.ai
log into Airtable
You do not need to deeply understand DNS. It’s mostly copy/paste into the domain dashboard.
