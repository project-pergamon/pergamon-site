Project Pergamon is currently looking for collaborators and early supporters in five areas:

  • Archival and library preservation — digital preservation, metadata, custody, and long-horizon stewardship
  • Technical preservation engineering — tape workflows, integrity checks, packaging, and archival infrastructure
  • AI model reconstruction and documentation — architecture, tokenizer, execution context, and reconstructability
  • Legal and governance counsel — licensing, access restrictions, provenance, and jurisdictional questions
  • Fundraising and institutional development — donor strategy, partnerships, and long-term institutional support

If your work touches the future custody of public AI, we would like to hear from you.
